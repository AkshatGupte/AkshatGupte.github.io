01 About

I'm a student who likes building things with machine learning, and I care more about whether a system holds up under measurement than whether the demo looks good.

Most of my recent work has been about evidence. I spent months testing whether attention weights in temporal models reflect genuine reasoning or just spurious correlation, using erasure, swap, and SHAP-based faithfulness experiments on 65k+ patient ICU records. The interesting part was not the headline accuracy, it was discovering the explanation metric was broken and fixing the architecture until it was not.

The same instinct shows up in the rest of my projects. A fine-tuned model that critiques Pokémon teams ships with a deterministic fact-checker that verifies every claim it makes. A natural-language analytics tool validates and retries its own generated SQL instead of trusting the first attempt. When I add a piece of infrastructure, it is usually because I watched something fail without it.

Outside of projects, I'm usually working through problems on LeetCode.
